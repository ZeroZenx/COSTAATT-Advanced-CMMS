## 💼 **What is CMMS?**

Our new **Computerized Maintenance Management System** helps manage all facility maintenance:

### **What You Can Do:**
- ✅ **Submit maintenance requests** online
- ✅ **Track work order status** in real-time
- ✅ **Manage assets** and equipment
- ✅ **Schedule preventive maintenance**
- ✅ **Monitor inventory** and supplies
- ✅ **Generate reports** and analytics
